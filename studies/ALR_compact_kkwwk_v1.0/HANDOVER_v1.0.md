# Compact KKWWK v1.0 handover

## Current conclusion

The 3-3-3-2-1 compact KKWWK pedestal works geometrically: the two lower K tiers can be placed vertically under the normal WWK head, and the resulting minimum pitch is effectively the same as a normal WWK head at the same dose/visibility (sub-micron difference in the sampled local model).

However, the five-tier height remains the dominant viewer-shadow penalty. Compact low-V variants are tighter than standard 5-4-3-2-1 KKWWK once the latter hits its 2.0 mm width floor, but none of the fine-verified compact cases simultaneously matches the current baseline dark band and improves contrast.

Useful compact region: approximately L0.20/V55 to L0.205/V50, S1.2, H0. These predict roughly 25-29% more brightness and about 2-3% better contrast than the current reference, but dark bands around 1.38-1.39 mm versus 1.044 mm baseline. Deliberately widening pitch by 0.05-0.10 mm raises contrast further but also widens the viewer dark band.

Same-pitch A1/B1 control: removing the outer K skirt does not change useful brightness, but increases ambient pickup and lowers contrast modestly. Thus the old 5-4 base provides ambient shielding rather than the brightness gain.

Next design question, if pursued: reduce viewer visibility of the lower pedestal itself (for example by changing pedestal height/placement/profile) rather than merely reducing its lateral road count. Do not assume that further lowering V will solve the viewer shadow; the sweep shows ambient rejection collapses first.

## Reference

Current common reference: PETG KWK, L0.20, S1.2, V70, H0, piece 1-2, v156 mapped-white local reconstruction.

## Limits

This is not a full curved-lattice regeneration and not a physical measurement. PETG material response uses the central v1.2 assumptions. See the report and included source dependencies.
