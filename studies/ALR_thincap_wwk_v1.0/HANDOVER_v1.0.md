# Thin-cap WWK handover v1.0

A 0.10 mm-dose K cap on full-height W tiers is a strong WWK improvement in the local v156 PETG model. At W0.24/K0.10/V60 it retains essentially full-cap WWK contrast while reducing mean pitch by 15.5% and viewer dark band by 18.8%, and raises brightness 15.2% versus full WWK.

For tighter viewer shadow, W0.24/K0.10/V40 gives +19.9% brightness / +1.75% contrast versus current KWK reference with a 1.246 mm band. W0.22/K0.10/V50 gives +34.8% brightness / +0.57% contrast with a 1.216 mm band. No contrast-retaining state tested beats the current reference 1.044 mm viewer band.

0.10 mm is the current cap-height knee; 0.08 and 0.12 mm are useful print neighbours. Explicit pitch probes show physical pitch/actual visible-white fraction, not nominal V alone, determines the state.

Analysis only; no printer converter/G-code was changed.
